`robo grasp-geometry --roi x0,y0,x1,y1 --floor_z M`
Free RGB-D fit returns `grasp_geometry`: pinch center, visible bounds, height and narrow-axis yaw candidates; assumes an isolated solid shape on a horizontal plane.
ROI uses full head-image pixels, exclusive ends, entire silhouette and surrounding plane; cut silhouettes, multiple surfaces, missing plane or invalid depth fail.
`robo roi-transfer left|right --roi x0,y0,x1,y1 --floor_z M --to_x M --to_y M --to_z M [--clearance 0.10] [--via x,y]`
`robo roi-check` takes the same arguments; free, motionless geometry fit and full-route IK search: vertical first, then ±30°/±45° tilts with horizontal closing axis; at most 50 candidates, both finger orientations.
`robo grasp-transfer left|right --x M --y M --z M --to_x M --to_y M --to_z M --floor_z M --roi x0,y0,x1,y1 [--open x|y] [--approach down|down45] [--yaw 0] [--clearance 0.10] [--via x,y]`
`robo transfer-check` takes the same arguments as `grasp-transfer`; free full-route IK preview. Yaw rotates the preset about world z in degrees; fitted yaw is an absolute closing-axis heading from world x.
Coordinates are absolute world meters; x/y/z is the source TCP pinch center, to_x/to_y/to_z the release center. Clearance is 0.06–0.18 m above the higher endpoint; heights exceed floor_z; via is an elevated waypoint.
Transfers preflight, gate on full-image quietness, refit/recheck, raise, rotate, open, descend, close, lift, check RGB-D, carry, lower, release and retreat; one command plus physical steps, no physical retries.
Returns `plan_ok`/`plan_fail_reason`, `grasp_geometry`, `preflight` (yaw/tilt, direction vectors, costs), `visual_gate`, `stages`, `visual_checks`, `released`, `action_steps`. Gate: 2 s quiet initially, 0.4 s after successful transfer; 6 s timeout (50–150 or 10–150 steps). Same robot handles and finite non-rewound clock retain initialization across intervening commands/free failures; gate/execution failure resets it.
Input/fit/IK failures prevent manipulation; tracking error, clipping, ambiguous RGB-D or episode end stops at current pose/grip. Final lowering alone may release <=min(0.04 m, clearance/2) high with <=0.012 m lateral/5° angular error, fresh retention, reachable hold/retreat and checked retargeting; reports elevated_release_preflight/release_height_offset_m. RGB-D fuses head/available wrists; source residue excludes z <= floor_z+0.006 m, any view can veto. Missing wrists use head only; geometry/retention are heuristic.
