`robo transfer-cycle {left,right} --x X --y Y --z Z --tx X --ty Y --tz Z --tip L [--pitch DEG] [--clearance M] [--dwell S] [--travel_pitch DEG] [--entry_offset M] [--reserve S] [--release_wait S] [--finish_home 0|1]`
`robo transfer-estimate` accepts the same arguments without motion or command-budget cost; returns waypoints, estimated_seconds, fits_estimate and reachability_checked=false. Timing is heuristic, not a bound or deadline guarantee; terminal joint movement is excluded (home_time_unaccounted=true).
Side grasp, lift, compensated tilt, replace, release and retreat; requires an upright rigid item, open active gripper and clear swept corridors. An obstructing inactive hand must be open/empty with a clear backward corridor; bounded automatic retraction is not full collision checking.
World metres: x/y/z is interior grasp centre; tx/ty/tz is tip at full tilt; tip is initial vertical distance above grasp (0.02–0.30). Pitch is signed world-Y rotation, magnitude 120–140°, default +120°; positive tilts toward +X. Full-tilt TCP must exceed grasp Z by 0.02 m.
Defaults/ranges: clearance 0.06 m (0.06–0.25); dwell 0.80 s (0.80–2); travel_pitch=0 required; entry_offset=0 or 0.08–0.25 m; reserve=0 nonnegative seconds outside the operation; release_wait=0.16 s (0.16–0.48); finish_home=0 or 1.
Default entry is overhead, clearing grasp Z+tip by 0.04 m and preserving initial altitude; retreat is vertical. Positive entry_offset selects front staging at y-entry_offset, z+clearance, separate lowering/horizontal insertion, upright loaded departures/returns through that plane, and horizontal withdrawal before rising.
Loaded lateral travel stays upright. Outward/reverse arcs compensate tip XY at adaptive endpoints with ≤2.5 mm nominal chord error; TCP stays ≥grasp Z+clearance+0.02 m through 90°, then interpolates to full tilt. Intermediate endpoints require 5 mm/1° accuracy; supplied geometry and clearance are unverified.
Dwell requires continuous measured 10 mm/1° accuracy; bounded extra settling is ≤0.24 s plus ≤3 ticks to finish already stable exposure. Recovery requires 10 mm/2° and ≤15 mm tip-error bound. Replacement stages 10 mm above grasp, then descends; both staging/release require 2 mm/0.5°. Release may wait ≤12 closed-hand ticks, extending each four-tick window only with ≥10% reduction in excess normalized error above tolerance; holds preserve estimated downstream time plus reserve.
Depth preflight rejects a narrow requested section when a coaxial lower fitted radius exceeds it by 1.5×; returns grasp_support and suggested_geometry without substitution. Missing/inconclusive depth reports checked=false and permits continuation. Opening continues during retreat; aperture/attachment are unmeasured. Withdrawal requires 10 mm/2° and ≤15 mm TCP-to-wrist displacement bound.
finish_home=1 withdraws before concurrent return of both empty open hands to base home joint targets; requires clear straight joint paths and checks time after release. Returns leading completion.home_verified, remaining_steps, repeated_home cost/fit and home_result; successful verification covers both home poses only.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, stages, estimated_seconds, inactive_retreat, tilt_hold_steps, completion_scope=motion_only, grasp_verified=false and transfer_verified=false. Invalid geometry/time, tracking or execution errors stop; failures after closing may leave the hand closed. Only an unmoved combined-aim IK failure permits one time-checked split fallback; no other retries.
